    Attendance (CBO & FCCN, SDY) — UAT Test Scenarios

    A complete acceptance-test walkthrough of the Attendance module in ECMS: 18 scenarios in 3 parts, covering CBO daily/weekly attendance and monthly certification, FCCN/SDY attendance (TITO does not apply here — it is tracked only in CAPS Online, and only for EDY seats), and the SDY+ enrollment indicator and reporting. Choose your role below to see the scenarios that apply to you, work through each one, and record Pass / Fail / Blocked with your sign-off.

    ECMS internal login (ECMS Internal Admin): https://doeecms--uat.sandbox.lightning.force.com/
    ECMS Vendor Portal login (Attendance Taker): https://doeecms--uat.sandbox.my.site.com/portal

    Who are you testing as?

    All statusesNot RunPassFailBlocked

    Results are saved automatically in this browser on this computer. Choose your role above and the checklist below will show only the scenarios that apply to you. Export results (CSV) sends everything you've recorded to the test lead.

    Two things to know before you start.

    1. Follow each scenario's steps exactly. Some steps ask you to click through a real action (like a validation that's expected to block Save) and confirm the real result. Others ask you to stop short of saving, so a shared example record stays usable for the next person. Do exactly what each step says, no more and no less.

    2. TITO is additional data, not a separate approval flow. Extended-day (TITO) attendance uses the exact same Attendance record and Draft → Submitted → Certified lifecycle as standard attendance — the only difference is the four Check-in/Check-out time fields captured on top.

    How to log in

    Every scenario below tells you which of these two logins to use. Follow the exact steps here instead of guessing — don't skip ahead.

    A. Internal ECMS (ECMS Internal Admin). Go to https://doeecms--uat.sandbox.lightning.force.com/ and sign in with the internal credentials you were given. This is the starting point for every scenario, including the ones that end up in the Vendor Portal — you always reach the Vendor Portal by starting here first.

    B. ECMS Vendor Portal (Attendance Taker). You won't have a separate vendor username/password — you reach the portal from inside internal ECMS, logged in as yourself, by “logging in as” the specific vendor contact the scenario names. Follow these steps exactly:

    1. While logged in to internal ECMS (step A), find that person's Contact record one of two ways:
      • Type their name into the global search bar at the top of the page and click their Contact result; or
      • Open the Site record the scenario mentions (scroll to find a Site link on the record you started from, and click it), then click the Site Contact name shown at the top of the Site record.
    2. On that Contact record, click Log in to Experience as User in the action bar near the top.
    3. This takes over your current browser tab — there is no new window. You'll land on the Vendor Portal home page, already signed in as that person (their name shows top-right). You are no longer signed in to internal ECMS in this tab while you're here.
    4. When you're done testing as the vendor and need to go back to internal ECMS, click the name/avatar in the top-right corner and choose Log Out, then navigate back to the internal ECMS URL from step A and sign back in.
