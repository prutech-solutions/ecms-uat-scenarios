    Budget Creation & Approval (CBO) — UAT Test Scenarios

    A Budget is created once per Site and Fiscal Year to define how contract funds will be allocated. After submission, the budget is reviewed by DOE staff and must be approved before certain downstream financial activities (like invoicing) can occur.

    Happy-path, rejection, and edge-case walkthrough of the Budget Creation & Approval process in ECMS: 20 scenarios in 5 parts, including the Pre-Approval path for budgets without a FAMIS Contract ID and its automatic transition once one is attached. Choose your role below to see the scenarios that apply to you, work through each one, and record Pass / Fail / Blocked with your sign-off.

    Four things to know before you start.

    1. Two logins, one process. Budgets are created by the CBO (Community-Based Organization) or FCCN (Family Childcare Network) in the ECMS Vendor Portal, then reviewed by DOE staff in the internal ECMS Lightning app — different URLs, different logins. The “Where to go” line on each scenario tells you which one to use.

    2. Refresh after every action. The Status field, the progress bar at the top of the budget, and the Approval History tab do not always update live — refresh the page after every Save, Submit, Approve or Reject.

    3. Some approval-chain steps are real clicks, others are dry runs by design. The Operations Analyst and Operations Director each see Approve/Reject buttons directly above the budget's Approval History. Each scenario tells you whether to click through for real (creating your own disposable budget first where needed, BUD-S01-S04) or to locate the button and stop short — follow that instruction exactly so a shared example stays usable for the next tester.

    4. Finding a budget at a given approval-chain stage. Open the Budgets tab → list view → “All”, then type the Status you need (e.g. “Approved by OA”) into the list’s own search box (top right) and press Enter — this only filters what you see, it doesn’t change the shared view for other testers. The search box matches text in any visible column, not just Status, so check the Status column on each result before picking one. If nothing matches, submit and advance your own budget first (Budget Creation module, BUD-S01-S04) to create a fresh one at that stage.

    Scope: happy path, OA/OD reject, and Information Requested are covered. Amendments to an already-approved budget and the third-level Central Approver check are out of scope for this version.

    How to log in

    Every scenario below tells you which of these two logins to use. Follow the exact steps here instead of guessing — don't skip ahead.

    A. Internal ECMS (Operations Analyst / Operations Director / ECMS Internal Admin). Go to https://doeecms--uat.sandbox.lightning.force.com/ and sign in with the internal credentials you were given. This is the starting point for every scenario, including the ones that end up in the Vendor Portal — you always reach the Vendor Portal by starting here first.

    B. ECMS Vendor Portal (Budget Manager). You won't have a separate vendor username/password — you reach the portal from inside internal ECMS, logged in as yourself, by “logging in as” the specific vendor contact the scenario names. Follow these steps exactly:

    1. While logged in to internal ECMS (step A), find that person's Contact record one of two ways:
      • Type their name into the global search bar at the top of the page and click their Contact result; or
      • Open the Site record the scenario mentions (scroll to find a Site link on the record you started from, and click it), then click the Site Contact name shown at the top of the Site record.
    2. On that Contact record, click Log in to Experience as User in the action bar near the top.
    3. This takes over your current browser tab — there is no new window. You'll land on the Vendor Portal home page, already signed in as that person (their name shows top-right). You are no longer signed in to internal ECMS in this tab while you're here.
    4. When you're done testing as the vendor and need to go back to internal ECMS, click the name/avatar in the top-right corner and choose Log Out, then navigate back to the internal ECMS URL from step A and sign back in.

    Scope. Happy path, OA/OD rejection (including the vendor-facing Sub Status visibility and resubmit-button behavior), Information Requested, and the Pre-Approval path for budgets without a FAMIS Contract ID are covered. There is no third-level Central Approval for Budgets — final approval is always OD-level (“Approved by DOE”). A budget on a Legacy Payment Terms (LPT) contract is created and approved exactly the same way as any other budget — Legacy's distinct invoicing schedule (September 20% OA-only advance, then 10%/month Vendor-only through June, with a District 84 attendance-certification exception) is a separate, invoice-side behavior covered in the Invoice Creation & Payment tracker's Part E, not here. Built from the ECMS Budgets & Invoicing BRD (D08) and screens already verified in the ECMS User Guide's Budget Creation & Approval guide.
