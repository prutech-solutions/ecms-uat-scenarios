    Budget Creation & Approval — UAT Test Scenarios

    Happy-path, rejection, and edge-case walkthrough of the Budget Creation & Approval process in ECMS: 18 scenarios in 5 parts, including the Pre-Approval path for budgets without a FAMIS Contract ID and its automatic transition once one is attached. Choose your role below to see the scenarios that apply to you, work through each one, and record Pass / Fail / Blocked with your sign-off.

    Four things to know before you start.

    1. Two logins, one process. Budgets are created by the CBO/FCCN in the ECMS Vendor Portal, then reviewed by DOE staff in the internal ECMS Lightning app — different URLs, different logins. The “Where to go” line on each scenario tells you which one to use.

    2. Refresh after every action. The Status field, the progress bar at the top of the budget, and the Approval History tab do not always update live — refresh the page after every Save, Submit, Approve or Reject.

    3. Some approval-chain steps are real clicks, others are dry runs by design. The Operations Analyst and Operations Director each see Approve/Reject buttons directly above the budget's Approval History. BUD-S06 (the happy-path OA approval) is a real click, since each tester can create their own disposable budget first (BUD-S01-S04) rather than consuming the shared example. BUD-S09, S10, S13, and S14 are deliberately dry runs (locate the button, don't click) because they act on budgets at stages where only 1–2 examples exist in this org and a real click would consume the only example for the next tester.

    4. Only one or two budgets exist at each approval-chain stage. B-00000087 (Submitted, awaiting OA), B-00000042/B-00000088 (OA-approved, awaiting OD), and B-00000021 (Information Requested) are each one of only 1–2 such records in this org. If you want to exercise one of these steps for real rather than as a dry run, submit and advance your own budget first (Budget Creation module, BUD-S01-S04) to create a fresh one at that stage.

    Scope: happy path, OA/OD reject, and Information Requested are covered. Amendments to an already-approved budget and the third-level Central Approver check are out of scope for this version.

    How to log in

    Every scenario below tells you which of these two logins to use. Follow the exact steps here instead of guessing — don't skip ahead.

    A. Internal ECMS (Operations Analyst / Operations Director / ECMS Internal Admin). Go to https://doeecms--uat.sandbox.lightning.force.com/ and sign in with the internal credentials you were given. This is the starting point for every scenario, including the ones that end up in the Vendor Portal — you always reach the Vendor Portal by starting here first.

    B. ECMS Vendor Portal (Budget Manager). You won't have a separate vendor username/password — you reach the portal from inside internal ECMS, logged in as yourself, by “logging in as” the specific vendor contact the scenario names. Follow these steps exactly:

    1. While logged in to internal ECMS (step A), find that person's Contact record one of two ways:
      • Type their name into the global search bar at the top of the page and click their Contact result; or
      • Open the Site record the scenario mentions (scroll to find a Site link on the record you started from, and click it), then click the Site Contact name shown at the top of the Site record.
    2. On that Contact record, click Log in to Experience as User in the action bar near the top.
    3. This takes over your current browser tab — there is no new window. You'll land on the Vendor Portal home page, already signed in as that person (their name shows top-right). You are no longer signed in to internal ECMS in this tab while you're here.
    4. When you're done testing as the vendor and need to go back to internal ECMS, click the name/avatar in the top-right corner and choose Log Out, then navigate back to the internal ECMS URL from step A and sign back in.
