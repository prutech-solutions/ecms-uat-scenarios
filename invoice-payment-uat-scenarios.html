    Invoice Creation & Payment (CBO) — UAT Test Scenarios

    A complete acceptance-test walkthrough of Invoice Creation & Payment in ECMS: 18 scenarios in 5 parts, covering regular monthly invoice creation and Operations Analyst (OA) / Operations Director (OD) approval, FAMIS funding hand-off and payment status tracking, Advance Payment Invoices and third-level Central Approval, and Legacy Budget invoicing (Legacy Payment Terms contracts). Choose your role below to see the scenarios that apply to you, work through each one, and record Pass / Fail / Blocked with your sign-off.

    Four things to know before you start.

    1. Two logins, one workflow. Invoice creation, editing, and submission happen in the ECMS Vendor Portal as an Invoice Manager (e.g. Damien Ortiz). OA/OD review, Central Approval, and FAMIS/payment-status detail are all reviewed in the internal ECMS Lightning app.

    2. The "Requires Central Approval" field drives the extra approval stage. Whenever a scenario says an invoice needs a third sign-off, that's driven by the EDY/HS-CTL seat content of the invoice, reflected in its "Requires Central Approval" field — check that field first if the approval stepper doesn't match what's expected.

    3. IPU-S06's Reassign step is a real, live execution. It finds an invoice at "Pending Approval" via a list-view filter, reassigns its Approval Request to a different Operations Analyst for real, and completes a real approval as the new assignee — this is a permanent action, not a dry run.

    4. Invoice edits are not auto-saved. If you navigate away, refresh the browser, click Cancel, or close the browser before clicking Save, any line items, amounts, or other changes you've entered will be lost. Click Save explicitly to keep your work.

    How to log in

    Every scenario below tells you which of these two logins to use. Follow the exact steps here instead of guessing — don't skip ahead.

    A. Internal ECMS (Operations Analyst / Operations Director / ECMS Internal Admin). Go to https://doeecms--uat.sandbox.lightning.force.com/ and sign in with the internal credentials you were given. This is the starting point for every scenario, including the ones that end up in the Vendor Portal — you always reach the Vendor Portal by starting here first.

    B. ECMS Vendor Portal (Invoice Manager). You won't have a separate vendor username/password — you reach the portal from inside internal ECMS, logged in as yourself, by “logging in as” the specific vendor contact the scenario names. Follow these steps exactly:

    1. While logged in to internal ECMS (step A), find that person's Contact record one of two ways:
      • Type their name into the global search bar at the top of the page and click their Contact result; or
      • Open the Site record the scenario mentions (scroll to find a Site link on the record you started from, and click it), then click the Site Contact name shown at the top of the Site record.
    2. On that Contact record, click Log in to Experience as User in the action bar near the top.
    3. This takes over your current browser tab — there is no new window. You'll land on the Vendor Portal home page, already signed in as that person (their name shows top-right). You are no longer signed in to internal ECMS in this tab while you're here.
    4. When you're done testing as the vendor and need to go back to internal ECMS, click the name/avatar in the top-right corner and choose Log Out, then navigate back to the internal ECMS URL from step A and sign back in.
