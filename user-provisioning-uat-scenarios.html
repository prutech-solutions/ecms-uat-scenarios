    User Provisioning & Setup — UAT Test Scenarios

    Happy-path verification walkthrough of User Provisioning & Setup in ECMS: 9 scenarios in 3 parts, covering CBO/FCCN vendor portal user setup through the Provision Contact wizard, internal DOE staff account setup, and access-integrity checks (new access never removes existing access, reports/homepage work immediately, FCCN site-scoped provisioning).     Three things to know before you start.

    1. The Provision Contact wizard is the real access mechanism. Run by an ECMS Internal Admin from a Contact record, this wizard — not a manually-created access-group assignment — is what actually creates a working portal login and grants ECMS functions and site scoping.

    2. Never actually change a real record. Every New/Edit/Provision step in this tracker is a dry run against a real, already-configured example (Damien Ortiz at Cedar Point Early Childhood Academy, Jordan the FCCN Network Director) — open the record or wizard, confirm what you see, then Cancel out. Don't click Save/Finish on a provisioning step for a record that isn't already set up that way.

    3. Internal and vendor accounts are set up differently. Internal DOE staff accounts are created manually in Setup > Users with a Profile, Role, and License. CBO/FCCN vendor accounts are set up through the Provision Contact wizard on their Contact record instead.

    Scope: CBO/FCCN vendor portal user setup through the Provision Contact wizard (Part A), internal DOE staff account setup (Part B), and access-integrity checks covering additive access grants, immediate reports/homepage access, and FCCN site-scoped provisioning (Part C).

    How to log in

    Every scenario below tells you which of these two logins to use. Follow the exact steps here instead of guessing — don't skip ahead.

    A. Internal ECMS (ECMS Internal Admin). Go to https://doeecms--uat.sandbox.lightning.force.com/ and sign in with the internal credentials you were given. This is the starting point for every scenario, including the ones that end up in the Vendor Portal — you always reach the Vendor Portal by starting here first.

    B. ECMS Vendor Portal (Vendor Admin). You won't have a separate vendor username/password — you reach the portal from inside internal ECMS, logged in as yourself, by “logging in as” the specific vendor contact the scenario names. Follow these steps exactly:

    1. While logged in to internal ECMS (step A), find that person's Contact record one of two ways:
      • Type their name into the global search bar at the top of the page and click their Contact result; or
      • Open the Site record the scenario mentions (scroll to find a Site link on the record you started from, and click it), then click the Site Contact name shown at the top of the Site record.
    2. On that Contact record, click Log in to Experience as User in the action bar near the top.
    3. This takes over your current browser tab — there is no new window. You'll land on the Vendor Portal home page, already signed in as that person (their name shows top-right). You are no longer signed in to internal ECMS in this tab while you're here.
    4. When you're done testing as the vendor and need to go back to internal ECMS, click the name/avatar in the top-right corner and choose Log Out, then navigate back to the internal ECMS URL from step A and sign back in.
