Why these stages, not a strict 1-12 order: Invoice genuinely cannot be created until its Budget is Approved and Attendance is certified, so Stage 3's internal order matters — but Budget Creation and Enrollments don't block each other and can be tested in either order within Stage 3. Similarly, Contracts & Seats and Seat Planning (Stage 2) are independent admin tasks, not a strict chain.

How these work. Every tracker is self-contained and interactive: pick your role from the dropdown, work through only the scenarios that apply to you, mark each one Pass / Fail / Blocked, and click Report Your Findings to send the result to the shared Smartsheet. Results are also saved automatically in your browser as you go, and can be exported to CSV at any time to send to the test lead.
