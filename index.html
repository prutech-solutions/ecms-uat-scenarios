How these work. Every tracker is self-contained and interactive: pick your role from the dropdown, work through only the scenarios that apply to you, mark each one Pass / Fail / Blocked, and sign off with your name. Results are saved automatically in your browser as you go, and can be exported to CSV at any time to send to the test lead.
