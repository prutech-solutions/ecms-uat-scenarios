    Enrollment Discharge Process — UAT Test Scenarios

    Happy-path verification walkthrough of the Enrollment Discharge Process in ECMS: 13 scenarios in 3 parts, covering how a CBO/FCCN vendor submits and DOE reviews a discharge request, how a completed discharge updates attendance and enrollment status, and the discharge-requested indicator's reliability. Note: requests to change a Class, Seat Type, or Program Type use the same underlying Enrollment Change Request wizard but are covered in the Enrollments tracker, not here. Choose your role below to see the scenarios that apply to you, work through each one, and record Pass / Fail / Blocked with your sign-off.

    ECMS internal login (Operations Analyst, Operations Director, ECMS Internal Admin, Enrollment Specialist): https://doeecms--uat.sandbox.lightning.force.com/
    ECMS Vendor Portal login (Enrollment Manager): https://doeecms--uat.sandbox.my.site.com/portal

    Three things to know before you start.

    1. One login for the request, a different one for the review. A discharge is requested by a CBO/FCCN Enrollment Manager in the ECMS Vendor Portal, then reviewed and approved by a DOE Enrollment Specialist in the internal ECMS Lightning app. Use “Log in to Experience as User” from a vendor Contact record to see the portal side, same tab, no popup.

    2. Never actually submit or save a change. Every step in this tracker is a dry run against real, already-processed examples (Camila Vasquez's Active enrollment ENROL-0000000520, Discharge Requests ENROL-CR-00000005/00000006, and discharged enrollments ENROL-0000000005/00000027/00000226) — open the record, confirm what you see, then Cancel out. Don't click Submit/Save on a new discharge request.

    3. Discharge Date drives the attendance rest-of-month update. Whenever a scenario talks about attendance switching to Discharged, that's driven by the Discharge Date on the Enrollment or its Discharge Request — check that field first if something looks wrong.

    Scope: discharge request submission, DOE review/approval, and the resulting attendance/enrollment-status updates already shown in the User Guide are covered, along with the discharge-requested indicator's reliability in Part C.

    How to log in

    Every scenario below tells you which of these two logins to use. Follow the exact steps here instead of guessing — don't skip ahead.

    A. Internal ECMS (Operations Analyst, Operations Director, ECMS Internal Admin, Enrollment Specialist). Go to https://doeecms--uat.sandbox.lightning.force.com/ and sign in with the internal credentials you were given. This is the starting point for every scenario, including the ones that end up in the Vendor Portal — you always reach the Vendor Portal by starting here first.

    B. ECMS Vendor Portal (Enrollment Manager). You won't have a separate vendor username/password — you reach the portal from inside internal ECMS, logged in as yourself, by “logging in as” the specific vendor contact the scenario names. Follow these steps exactly:

    1. While logged in to internal ECMS (step A), find that person's Contact record one of two ways:
      • Type their name into the global search bar at the top of the page and click their Contact result; or
      • Open the Site record the scenario mentions (scroll to find a Site link on the record you started from, and click it), then click the Site Contact name shown at the top of the Site record.
    2. On that Contact record, click Log in to Experience as User in the action bar near the top.
    3. This takes over your current browser tab — there is no new window. You'll land on the Vendor Portal home page, already signed in as that person (their name shows top-right). You are no longer signed in to internal ECMS in this tab while you're here.
    4. When you're done testing as the vendor and need to go back to internal ECMS, click the name/avatar in the top-right corner and choose Log Out, then navigate back to the internal ECMS URL from step A and sign back in.
