    FCCN Network & Provider Setup — UAT Test Scenarios

    Happy-path verification walkthrough of FCCN Network & Provider Setup in ECMS: 26 scenarios in 6 parts, covering Family Child Care Network account setup, affiliating individual home-based FCC Providers, FCCN staff portal access, coaching/site assignment for Education Specialists and Education Directors, site closure & reopen, vendor/provider staff tracking, and incident reporting & management (shared with the Vendor & Site Setup (CBO) tracker, so FCCN testers don’t need a separate guide for these). Network and site calendar setup has its own FCCN Calendar Management tracker. Choose your role below to see the scenarios that apply to you, work through each one, and record Pass / Fail / Blocked and report your findings.
