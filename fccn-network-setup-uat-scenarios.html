    FCCN Network & Provider Setup — UAT Test Scenarios

    Happy-path verification walkthrough of FCCN Network & Provider Setup in ECMS: 9 scenarios in 3 parts, covering Family Child Care Network account setup, affiliating individual home-based FCC Providers, FCCN staff portal access, and coaching/site assignment for Education Specialists and Education Directors. Network and site calendar setup has its own FCCN Calendar Management tracker. Choose your role below to see the scenarios that apply to you, work through each one, and record Pass / Fail / Blocked with your sign-off.

    ECMS internal login (Operations Analyst, ECMS Internal Admin, FCCN Affiliation Management): https://doeecms--uat.sandbox.lightning.force.com/
    ECMS Vendor Portal login (FCCN staff, Education Specialist, Education Director): https://doeecms--uat.sandbox.my.site.com/portal

    Two things to know before you start.

    1. A network has no site of its own — its roster of Providers is the "site." An FCCN is its own organization record, just like its affiliated FCC Providers, connected by a single "Affiliated Network" field rather than a separate linking record. Network/Provider account creation, the affiliation link, and contact records are edited in the internal ECMS Lightning app by an Operations Analyst, ECMS Internal Admin, or a member of the FCCN Affiliation Management team. To see what the network's own staff sees, use “Log in to Experience as User” from their Contact record — same tab, no popup.

    2. Never actually save a change. Every New/Edit step in this tracker is a dry run against a real, already-configured example (Riverbend Family Childcare Network, Riverbend FCC Provider - Woodlawn, its FCCN Network Director contact) — open the record, confirm what you see, then Cancel out. Don't click Save/Submit on a new or duplicate record.

    Scope: FCCN/Provider account setup, affiliation, staff contacts, and portal access already shown in the User Guide are covered.

    How to log in

    Every scenario below tells you which of these two logins to use. Follow the exact steps here instead of guessing — don't skip ahead.

    A. Internal ECMS (Operations Analyst, ECMS Internal Admin, FCCN Affiliation Management). Go to https://doeecms--uat.sandbox.lightning.force.com/ and sign in with the internal credentials you were given. This is the starting point for every scenario, including the ones that end up in the Vendor Portal — you always reach the Vendor Portal by starting here first.

    B. ECMS Vendor Portal (FCCN staff). You won't have a separate vendor username/password — you reach the portal from inside internal ECMS, logged in as yourself, by “logging in as” the specific network contact the scenario names. Follow these steps exactly:

    1. While logged in to internal ECMS (step A), find that person's Contact record one of two ways:
      • Type their name into the global search bar at the top of the page and click their Contact result; or
      • Open the FCCN's Account record and click their name in the related Contacts list.
    2. On that Contact record, click Log in to Experience as User in the action bar near the top.
    3. This takes over your current browser tab — there is no new window. You'll land on the Vendor Portal home page, already signed in as that person (their name shows top-right). You are no longer signed in to internal ECMS in this tab while you're here.
    4. When you're done testing as the vendor and need to go back to internal ECMS, click the name/avatar in the top-right corner and choose Log Out, then navigate back to the internal ECMS URL from step A and sign back in.
