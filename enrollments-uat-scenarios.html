    Enrollments (CBO, FCCN & Summer) — UAT Test Scenarios

    A complete acceptance-test walkthrough of the Enrollments module in ECMS: 47 scenarios in 6 parts, covering the CBO Draft→Submit→Approve lifecycle, FCCN network-staff differences, the mostly-automated Summer enrollment lifecycle, Admit Code/ACCIS eligibility/auto-approval mechanics, Enrollment Change Requests & discharges, and the CBO-only SDY+ tracker. Choose your role below to see the scenarios that apply to you, work through each one, and record Pass / Fail / Blocked with your sign-off.

    Three things to know before you start.

    1. Two logins, same enrollment record. CBO/FCCN Enrollment Managers and Network Directors work Draft/Submitted enrollments in the ECMS Vendor Portal. DOE Enrollment Specialists review, approve, reject, or request more information from the internal ECMS Lightning app. Several scenarios below ask you to check the same record from both sides.

    2. Follow each scenario's steps exactly. Some steps ask you to click through a real action (like Reassign, or attempting a Submit that's expected to be blocked) and confirm the real result. Others ask you to stop short of saving, so a shared example record stays usable for the next person. Do exactly what each step says, no more and no less.

    3. A few terms used throughout: CBO (Community-Based Organization) and FCCN (Family Child Care Network) are the two kinds of vendor organizations ECMS supports. The caret is the small dropdown arrow (▾) next to a list view's name — click it to switch which list/filter you're viewing.

    How to log in

    Every scenario below tells you which of these two logins to use. Follow the exact steps here instead of guessing — don't skip ahead.

    A. Internal ECMS (DOE Enrollment Specialist / ECMS Admin). Go to https://doeecms--uat.sandbox.lightning.force.com/ and sign in with the internal credentials you were given. This is the starting point for every scenario, including the ones that end up in the Vendor Portal — you always reach the Vendor Portal by starting here first.

    B. ECMS Vendor Portal (CBO/FCCN staff). You won't have a separate vendor username/password — you reach the portal from inside internal ECMS, logged in as yourself, by “logging in as” the specific vendor contact the scenario names. Follow these steps exactly:

    1. While logged in to internal ECMS (step A), find that person's Contact record one of two ways:
      • Type their name into the global search bar at the top of the page and click their Contact result; or
      • Open the Site record the scenario mentions (scroll to find a Site link on the enrollment/record you started from, and click it), then click the Site Contact name shown at the top of the Site record.
    2. On that Contact record, click Log in to Experience as User in the action bar near the top.
    3. This takes over your current browser tab — there is no new window. You'll land on the Vendor Portal home page, already signed in as that person (their name shows top-right). You are no longer signed in to internal ECMS in this tab while you're here.
    4. To find a specific enrollment from here, click Student Enrollments in the top nav, then click that record's Enrollment # link in the list (not the Student name link, which opens a different page).
    5. When you're done testing as the vendor and need to go back to internal ECMS, click the name/avatar in the top-right corner and choose Log Out, then navigate back to the internal ECMS URL from step A and sign back in.

    This same path works for both CBO staff and FCCN Network staff — only the Contact name you search for or the Site you start from changes.
