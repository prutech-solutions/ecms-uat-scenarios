    Vendor & Site Setup (CBO) — UAT Test Scenarios

    A complete acceptance-test walkthrough of Vendor & Site Setup (CBO) in ECMS: 21 scenarios in 4 parts, covering CBO account/site/contact/portal setup, site closure & reopen, vendor staff tracking, and incident reporting & management. Choose your role below to see the scenarios that apply to you, work through each one, and record Pass / Fail / Blocked with your sign-off.

    ECMS internal login (Operations Analyst, ECMS Internal Admin, Incident Management Team): https://doeecms--uat.sandbox.lightning.force.com/
    ECMS Vendor Portal login (Site Manager, Budget Manager, Invoice Manager, Attendance Taker): https://doeecms--uat.sandbox.my.site.com/portal

    Two things to know before you start.

    1. One login for setup, a different one for what the vendor sees. Account/Site/Contact/Site Staff creation and closure fields are edited in the internal ECMS Lightning app by an Operations Analyst or ECMS Internal Admin. To see what a CBO's own staff sees, use “Log in to Experience as User” from their Contact record — same tab, no popup (see How to log in below).

    2. Site Closure Status drives everything downstream. Whenever a scenario says a site “blocks” Enrollment, Budget, Invoice, or Attendance, that's driven entirely by the Site Master's Site Closure Status and Closure Date — check that field first if something looks wrong.

    How to log in

    Every scenario below tells you which of these two logins to use. Follow the exact steps here instead of guessing — don't skip ahead.

    A. Internal ECMS (Operations Analyst / ECMS Internal Admin). Go to https://doeecms--uat.sandbox.lightning.force.com/ and sign in with the internal credentials you were given. This is the starting point for every scenario, including the ones that end up in the Vendor Portal — you always reach the Vendor Portal by starting here first.

    B. ECMS Vendor Portal (Site Manager / Budget Manager / Invoice Manager / Attendance Taker). You won't have a separate vendor username/password — you reach the portal from inside internal ECMS, logged in as yourself, by “logging in as” the specific vendor contact the scenario names. Follow these steps exactly:

    1. While logged in to internal ECMS (step A), find that person's Contact record one of two ways:
      • Type their name into the global search bar at the top of the page and click their Contact result; or
      • Open the Site record the scenario mentions (scroll to find a Site link on the record you started from, and click it), then click the Site Contact name shown at the top of the Site record.
    2. On that Contact record, click Log in to Experience as User in the action bar near the top.
    3. This takes over your current browser tab — there is no new window. You'll land on the Vendor Portal home page, already signed in as that person (their name shows top-right). You are no longer signed in to internal ECMS in this tab while you're here.
    4. When you're done testing as the vendor and need to go back to internal ECMS, click the name/avatar in the top-right corner and choose Log Out, then navigate back to the internal ECMS URL from step A and sign back in.
