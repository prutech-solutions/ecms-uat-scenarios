    Seat Planning — UAT Test Scenarios

    Verification walkthrough of the Seat Planning app in ECMS: 5 scenarios in 1 part, covering the annual draft/edit/publish lifecycle for the FY Seat Planning app - grounded in a real FY2026 Draft and the FY2027 plan that has already gone Published in this UAT org. Choose your role below to see the scenarios that apply to you, work through each one, and record Pass / Fail / Blocked with your sign-off.

    ECMS internal login (Operations Analyst, Operations Director, Portfolio Team Member, Enrollment Specialist): https://doeecms--uat.sandbox.lightning.force.com/

    All statusesNot RunPassFailBlocked

    Results are saved automatically in this browser on this computer. Choose your role above and the checklist below will show only the scenarios that apply to you. Export results (CSV) sends everything you've recorded to the test lead.

    Two things to know before you start.

    1. This is an annual, org-wide process, not a per-vendor one. The Seat Planning app is worked entirely in the internal ECMS Lightning app by a Portfolio Team Member, with Operations Analysts, Operations Directors, and Enrollment Specialists also needing visibility. A single Draft or Published plan covers every CBO/FCCN vendor's seats at once, not just one vendor.

    2. Never actually click Finalize or Publish, and never save a change. Every step in this tracker is a dry run against real, already-set-up examples (the FY2026 Draft and the FY2027 plan already Published in this org) — open the record, confirm what you see, then Cancel out. Clicking Finalize/Publish on the shared FY2026 draft would affect every other tester using this org.

    Scope: the FY Seat Planning app's full Draft → Published lifecycle, access, and notifications are covered. Contract/period/seat creation itself, and day-to-day cost-per-child/seat-quantity/capacity maintenance, are covered in the Contracts & Seats tracker. The DOE's one-time Vendor Site Mapping data migration is tracked separately and is not yet in a testable state.

    How to log in

    Every scenario below uses the same internal ECMS login — there's no Vendor Portal side to Seat Planning.

    Internal ECMS (Operations Analyst, Operations Director, Portfolio Team Member, Enrollment Specialist). Go to https://doeecms--uat.sandbox.lightning.force.com/ and sign in with the internal credentials you were given.
