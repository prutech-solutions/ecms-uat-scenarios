    FCCN Calendar Management — UAT Test Scenarios

    Verification walkthrough of FCCN calendar management in ECMS: 5 scenarios in 2 parts, covering a network's shared default calendar, a Provider site requesting its own alternative calendar, and the DOE-side approval process for that request. Choose your role below to see the scenarios that apply to you, work through each one, and record Pass / Fail / Blocked with your sign-off.

    ECMS internal login (Operations Analyst, Operations Director, ECMS Internal Admin): https://doeecms--uat.sandbox.lightning.force.com/
    ECMS Vendor Portal login (Network Director/Vendor Admin, Affiliation Manager): https://doeecms--uat.sandbox.my.site.com/portal

    Three things to know before you start.

    1. A network calendar and a site calendar are separate records on the same object. Record Type “Network” (Site left blank) is the network-wide default; Record Type “Site” (Site set) is a single Provider's own override of that default, subject to DOE approval. Both are edited in the internal ECMS Lightning app or the vendor portal, depending on persona.

    2. Never actually save a change. Every New/Edit step in this tracker is a dry run against real, already-configured examples (the Queensview Family Childcare Network's Approved network calendar, and a Submitted site-level calendar request at FCCN_Site_Nivi) — open the record, confirm what you see, then Cancel out. Don't click Save/Submit on a new or duplicate record, and don't change the Status on the shared example records.

    3. Part B's last scenario depends on real workflow state, not guaranteed Pass. No Site-type calendar in UAT has yet reached Approved status, so the override-takes-effect scenario (CAL-S05) may come back Blocked until one does — that's expected, not a defect to report.

    Scope: network/site calendar creation and the DOE approval workflow are covered here. FCCN account/Provider/affiliation setup itself is covered in the FCCN Network & Provider Setup tracker.

    How to log in

    Every scenario below tells you which of these two logins to use. Follow the exact steps here instead of guessing — don't skip ahead.

    A. Internal ECMS (Operations Analyst, Operations Director, ECMS Internal Admin). Go to https://doeecms--uat.sandbox.lightning.force.com/ and sign in with the internal credentials you were given. This is the starting point for every scenario, including the ones that end up in the Vendor Portal — you always reach the Vendor Portal by starting here first.

    B. ECMS Vendor Portal (Network Director/Vendor Admin, Affiliation Manager). You won't have a separate vendor username/password — you reach the portal from inside internal ECMS, logged in as yourself, by “logging in as” the specific network contact the scenario names. Follow these steps exactly:

    1. While logged in to internal ECMS (step A), find that person's Contact record one of two ways:
      • Type their name into the global search bar at the top of the page and click their Contact result; or
      • Open the FCCN's Account record and click their name in the related Contacts list.
    2. On that Contact record, click Log in to Experience as User in the action bar near the top.
    3. This takes over your current browser tab — there is no new window. You'll land on the Vendor Portal home page, already signed in as that person (their name shows top-right). You are no longer signed in to internal ECMS in this tab while you're here.
    4. When you're done testing as the vendor and need to go back to internal ECMS, click the name/avatar in the top-right corner and choose Log Out, then navigate back to the internal ECMS URL from step A and sign back in.
