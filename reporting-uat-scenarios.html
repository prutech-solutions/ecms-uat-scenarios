    Reporting — UAT Test Scenarios

    Reporting now also has its own ECMS User Guide chapter (Reporting & Dashboards): 16 scenarios in 3 parts, covering the prebuilt attendance/enrollment/seat-capacity/budget/invoice dashboards and reports internal DOE staff already have, enhanced FCCN attendance reporting, the refresh/export/subscribe actions around them, and ECMS Internal Admin-only dashboard/report building. Choose your role below to see the scenarios that apply to you, work through each one, and record Pass / Fail / Blocked with your sign-off.

    ECMS internal login (ECMS Internal Admin, Enrollment Specialist, Operations Analyst, Operations Director): https://doeecms--uat.sandbox.lightning.force.com/

    Results are saved automatically in this browser on this computer. Choose your role above and the checklist below will show only the scenarios that apply to you. Export results (CSV) sends everything you've recorded to the test lead.

    Three things to know before you start.

    1. Internal ECMS only — there is no vendor-portal side to this module. Reporting is used by ECMS Internal Admins, Enrollment Specialists, Operations Analysts, and Operations Directors inside the internal ECMS Lightning app. CBO/vendor staff do not have a Reporting screen of their own, so every scenario below logs in once, internally.

    2. Never actually create or save anything new. Viewing existing dashboards and reports (Parts A and B) is genuinely read-only. Building a new dashboard or report (Part C) is a dry run — walk through naming it, adding widgets or columns, and reaching the Save/Create dialog, then Cancel out instead of confirming, so the shared folders stay clean for every tester.

    Scope: the prebuilt attendance/enrollment/seat-capacity/Head Start/EDY/budget/invoice dashboards and reports, and the refresh/download/subscribe actions around them, are covered in Parts A and B. Building new dashboards/reports, Enrollment Specialist report access, and two enrollment/attendance reports also reachable from the Enrollment and Attendance trackers are covered in Part C.

    How to log in

    Every scenario below is tested from the same internal ECMS login — there's no Vendor Portal side to Reporting.

    Internal ECMS (ECMS Internal Admin, Enrollment Specialist, Operations Analyst, Operations Director). Go to https://doeecms--uat.sandbox.lightning.force.com/ and sign in with the internal credentials you were given.
