    Contracts & Seats — UAT Test Scenarios

    A complete acceptance-test walkthrough of Contracts & Seats in ECMS: 16 scenarios in 2 parts, covering contract/period/seat creation and validation, ongoing cost-per-child and seat-quantity accuracy, and class-level seat capacity vs. occupancy — grounded in Cedar Point Early Childhood Academy's real contract chain. The annual FY Seat Planning app and Vendor Site Mapping now have their own Seat Planning tracker. Choose your role below to see the scenarios that apply to you, work through each one, and record Pass / Fail / Blocked with your sign-off.

    Two things to know before you start.

    1. One login for setup, a different one for what the vendor sees. Contracts, contract periods, and contract seats are all worked in the internal ECMS Lightning app by an Operations Analyst, Operations Director, or Portfolio Team Member. A few capacity/enrollment scenarios also check the ECMS Vendor Portal as a Site Manager would see it (see How to log in below).

    2. A seat's numbers flow straight into budgeting. A contract seat's Quantity × Cost Per Child is its Total Funding, and that number is exactly what a site's budget gets built and reconciled against — getting Quantity and Cost Per Child right at the contract-seat level matters well beyond this module.

    How to log in

    Every scenario below tells you which of these two logins to use. Follow the exact steps here instead of guessing — don't skip ahead.

    A. Internal ECMS (Operations Analyst / Operations Director / Portfolio Team Member / ECMS Internal Admin). Go to https://doeecms--uat.sandbox.lightning.force.com/ and sign in with the internal credentials you were given. This is the starting point for every scenario, including the ones that end up in the Vendor Portal — you always reach the Vendor Portal by starting here first.

    B. ECMS Vendor Portal (Site Manager). You won't have a separate vendor username/password — you reach the portal from inside internal ECMS, logged in as yourself, by “logging in as” the specific vendor contact the scenario names. Follow these steps exactly:

    1. While logged in to internal ECMS (step A), find that person's Contact record one of two ways:
      • Type their name into the global search bar at the top of the page and click their Contact result; or
      • Open the Site record the scenario mentions (scroll to find a Site link on the record you started from, and click it), then click the Site Contact name shown at the top of the Site record.
    2. On that Contact record, click Log in to Experience as User in the action bar near the top.
    3. This takes over your current browser tab — there is no new window. You'll land on the Vendor Portal home page, already signed in as that person (their name shows top-right). You are no longer signed in to internal ECMS in this tab while you're here.
    4. When you're done testing as the vendor and need to go back to internal ECMS, click the name/avatar in the top-right corner and choose Log Out, then navigate back to the internal ECMS URL from step A and sign back in.
